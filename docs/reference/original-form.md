## Microsoft interface text

When you submit this form, it will not automatically collect your details like name and email address unless you provide it yourself.

Microsoft also displays platform-specific privacy, terms, abuse-reporting, and branding text. These are platform UI, separate from the form author's content.

## Capacity behavior to settle

The original attendance options include declining and bringing a friend. Proposed behavior: declining consumes no seats; bringing a friend consumes two seats, managed together. A two-person party needs two available seats to confirm or claim a vacancy.
